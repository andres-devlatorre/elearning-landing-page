# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: subject-matter experts (SMEs) who hold valuable knowledge and need it turned into a finished course, but are not instructional designers or video producers. They land on the page deciding whether this studio can take their expertise and produce a course without the process becoming a burden.

Secondary: corporate buyers (L&D, HR, training managers) commissioning courses for their teams.

The page speaks to the SME first; the corporate buyer should still feel served, not alienated.

## Product Purpose

An e-learning production studio that produces courses, training videos and interactive modules. The landing page exists to get visitors to book a discovery call (Calendly). Success is a booked call from a qualified SME or buyer.

## Positioning

The studio has a proven, repeatable way to create courses that is easy for the client, fast in production, and yields a course that is easy for learners to digest. The claim is the combination of an easy process, speed, and an easy-to-digest result. Founder-led (the founder is the face of the studio).

## Operating Context

Visitors typically compare a few studios before booking a call. Proof is delivered through YouTube videos: one studio trailer and three client testimonial videos. Booking happens through a Calendly popup. Content is bilingual-relevant for the studio's wider work (localization is a service); the page itself is currently English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services offered: custom course production; training video and motion; interactive and SCORM modules for any LMS; localization and ongoing updates. Process: brief and strategy, script and produce, launch and measure.

Page is static (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`), no build step, hosted on a static host (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or Vercel). Fonts from Google Fonts; Calendly widget external.

Undecided / still placeholder: studio name, founder name and bio, contact details, social links, Calendly URL, YouTube IDs, client names, and all numeric claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Real trailer video, three real client testimonial videos, and real client names exist but have not yet been entered into the page. Until they are, the current stats (10+ years, 150+ courses, 500k learners, 60+ clients), metrics (92% completion, 40% faster ramp-up, 12k learners), quotes and client names are placeholders and must not be treated or presented as verified facts. No founder portrait (`assets/portrait.jpg`) or share image (`assets/og-image.jpg`) exists yet.

## Product Principles

1. Make the process feel easy: the studio's promise is low effort for the expert, so the page itself must be easy to read and act on.
2. Speed and clarity over spectacle: show a fast, proven path from knowledge to finished course.
3. Proof over assertion: lead with the real videos and clients; never ship unverified numbers or quotes.
4. Founder-led and human: the founder's presence is part of the trust.
5. One clear action: book a call.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement stated. Default to WCAG 2.1 AA, keyboard-operable navigation and video embeds, and reduced-motion support.
