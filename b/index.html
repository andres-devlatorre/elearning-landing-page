What we make

Complete online courses

Planning, scripts and the full build, shaped around what your learners need to be able to do.

  • We plan the learning goals and structure with you
  • We write every script, so you don't have to
  • Delivered ready for your course platform

Video and animation

Studio filming, animation and explainers that make ideas stick.

  • We direct every take, even if you've never been on camera
  • Animation and voice-over if you'd rather stay off screen
  • Short explainers for the hardest ideas

Interactive lessons

Scenarios, quizzes and simulations that work on any course platform and track each learner's progress. (In the trade: SCORM modules for your LMS.)

  • Quizzes that check understanding as learners go
  • Scenarios that let learners practise real decisions
  • Progress tracking in the platform you already use

Translation and updates

Subtitles, voice-over and translations, plus refreshes to keep your course current.

  • Subtitles and voice-over in more languages
  • Full translations of lessons and materials
  • Updates when your subject or tools change

I've never recorded anything. Can I still do this?

Yes. You don't need any experience. We plan it with you, write the script and direct every take, so you only need to know your subject.

How much of my time will it take?

Usually one planning call, a recording session if you choose to appear on camera, and time to review drafts. We handle everything in between.

Do I need slides or a script?

No. We can start from notes, slides, a book or simply a conversation. Writing the script is part of our job.

What if I don't want to be on camera?

We can build the course with animation and voice-over instead, so it works without you on screen.

Can the course be in more than one language?

Yes. We add subtitles, voice-over and full translations.
